PYSEC-2019-232 PyPI/tensorflow-gpu — Memcpy parameter overlap in Google Snappy library 1.1.4, as used in Google TensorFlow before 1.7.1, 
package: tensorflow-gpu
ecosystem: PyPI
versions affected:
  >= 0, < 1.7.1 (ECOSYSTEM)
aliases: CVE-2018-7577, GHSA-qx2v-j445-g354
references:
  https://github.com/tensorflow/tensorflow/blob/master/tensorflow/security/advisory/tfsa-2018-005.md
  https://github.com/advisories/GHSA-qx2v-j445-g354

Memcpy parameter overlap in Google Snappy library 1.1.4, as used in Google TensorFlow before 1.7.1, could result in a crash or read from other parts of process memory.
