PYSEC-2026-24 PyPI/apache-airflow-providers-smtp — Apache Airflow's SMTP provider `SmtpHook` called Python's `smtplib.SMTP.starttls()` without an SSL c
package: apache-airflow-providers-smtp
ecosystem: PyPI
versions affected:
  >= 2.0.0, < 3.0.0 (ECOSYSTEM)

references:

  https://github.com/advisories/GHSA-x8mh-94wc-33gv

Apache Airflow's SMTP provider `SmtpHook` called Python's `smtplib.SMTP.starttls()` without an SSL context, so no certificate validation was performed on the TLS upgrade. A man-in-the-middle between the Airflow worker and the SMTP server could present a self-signed certificate, complete the STARTTLS upgrade, and capture the SMTP credentials sent during the subsequent `login()` call. Users are advised to upgrade to the `apache-airflow-providers-smtp` version that contains the fix.
