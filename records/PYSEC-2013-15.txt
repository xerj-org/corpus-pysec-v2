PYSEC-2013-15 PyPI/salt — The salt master in Salt (aka SaltStack) 0.11.0 through 0.17.0 does not properly drop group privilege
package: salt
ecosystem: PyPI
versions affected:
  >= 0.11.0, < 0.17.1 (ECOSYSTEM)
aliases: CVE-2013-6617, GHSA-7wx3-vr2f-6p29
references:
  http://docs.saltstack.com/topics/releases/0.17.1.html
  https://github.com/advisories/GHSA-7wx3-vr2f-6p29

The salt master in Salt (aka SaltStack) 0.11.0 through 0.17.0 does not properly drop group privileges, which makes it easier for remote attackers to gain privileges.
