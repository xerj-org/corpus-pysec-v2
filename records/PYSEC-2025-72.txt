PYSEC-2025-72 PyPI/num2words — After a successful phishing attack, new versions of `num2words` were published containing malware.
package: num2words
ecosystem: PyPI
versions affected:
  0.5.15, 0.5.16
references:
  https://nitter.tiekoetter.com/SFLinux/status/1949906299308953827
  https://www.stepsecurity.io/blog/supply-chain-security-alert-num2words-pypi-package-shows-signs-of-compromise

The `num2words` project was compromised via a phishing attack
and two new versions were uploaded to PyPI containing malicious code.
The affected versions have been removed from PyPI,
and users are advised to remove the affected versions from their environments.
