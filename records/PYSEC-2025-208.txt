PYSEC-2025-208 PyPI/torch — A buffer overflow occurs in pytorch v2.7.0 when a PyTorch model consists of torch.nn.Conv2d, torch.n
package: torch
ecosystem: PyPI
versions affected:
  >= 0, < 2.7.1 (ECOSYSTEM)
aliases: CVE-2025-55558
references:
  https://gist.github.com/shaoyuyoung/0e7d2a586297ae9c8ed14d8706749efc
  https://github.com/pytorch/pytorch/issues/151523
  https://github.com/pytorch/pytorch/pull/151887

A buffer overflow occurs in pytorch v2.7.0 when a PyTorch model consists of torch.nn.Conv2d, torch.nn.functional.hardshrink, and torch.Tensor.view-torch.mv() and is compiled by Inductor, leading to a Denial of Service (DoS).
