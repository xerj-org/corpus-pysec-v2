PYSEC-2019-225 PyPI/tensorflow-cpu — Memcpy parameter overlap in Google Snappy library 1.1.4, as used in Google TensorFlow before 1.7.1, 
package: tensorflow-cpu
ecosystem: PyPI
versions affected:
  >= 0, < 1.7.1 (ECOSYSTEM)
aliases: CVE-2018-7577
references:
  https://github.com/tensorflow/tensorflow/blob/master/tensorflow/security/advisory/tfsa-2018-005.md

Memcpy parameter overlap in Google Snappy library 1.1.4, as used in Google TensorFlow before 1.7.1, could result in a crash or read from other parts of process memory.
